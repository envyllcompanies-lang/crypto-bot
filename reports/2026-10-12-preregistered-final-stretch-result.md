## Read this before the numbers

- 0 closed paper trades in total, spread over many variants. Treat any variant with fewer than 30 trades as an anecdote. Positions still open in a running session are not counted until they close.
- Fills are simulated: our orders land after the delay on the curve other traders left, with our own price impact, but nobody reacts to our trades.
- V1's X-link and top-5 holder checks are looked up once per candidate; entries wait for the answer, and a failed lookup leaves those checks not evaluated. 'Few tracked wallets' allows at most 2 holders from the tracked-wallet list (tracked.py, rebuilt nightly from recorded trades); until the first list exists it is not evaluated. Each variant starts with a 1 SOL wallet.
- Proxies: 'pro traders' are wallets that bought at least 0.5 SOL of the token; a bundle launch candle is a run to 2x launch before any sell; dollar levels assume V1's launch at $4,500.
- Flagged trades were closed by graduation or by the end of the run (valued by selling into the last curve), or overlapped a stream gap or a missed trade; their exit price is less certain.
- Final Stretch takes no entries in a run's first 40 minutes, because the column's market-cap ranking needs every token up to 40 minutes old.
- The funnel counts tokens whose rules passed at least once; the gates table logs only changes between pass and fail.
- Conviction (conviction.py) is our own proposal on top of V1, not V1's rule. Its weights and tier cut-offs are first guesses to be tuned on these results. Its points come from structural signals with published evidence (copycat, creator record, early-buyer share, trades to climb the curve, dev and insider shares, holder checks). The LLM narrative score (Groq, then Gemini, free tiers) adds no points. `conviction` ignores it; `conviction_narrative` uses it only to cap the tier (1-2 never buy, 3 at most average, 4 at most good, only 5 very high) and waits for the verdict before any entry. Comparing the two shows whether the gate helps.
- Copycats are counted against launches seen since the run started, so a run's first 24 hours find fewer of them.
