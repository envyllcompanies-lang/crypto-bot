## Read this before the numbers

- 179 closed paper trades in total, spread over many variants. Treat any variant with fewer than 30 trades as an anecdote.
- Fills are simulated: our orders land after the delay on the curve other traders left, with our own price impact, but nobody reacts to our trades.
- V1 checks not evaluated (they need extra RPC calls or metadata): x_link_is_community, top5_sol_balances_differ, top5_funding_not_clustered, few_tracked_wallets. Results are V1 without them.
- Proxies: 'pro traders' are wallets that bought at least 0.5 SOL of the token; a bundle launch candle is a run to 2x launch before any sell; dollar levels assume V1's launch at $4,500.
- Flagged trades were closed by graduation or by the end of the run (valued by selling into the last curve), or overlapped a stream gap or a missed trade; their exit price is less certain.
- Final Stretch takes no entries in a run's first 40 minutes, because the column's market-cap ranking needs every token up to 40 minutes old.
- The funnel counts tokens whose rules passed at least once; the gates table logs only changes between pass and fail.
