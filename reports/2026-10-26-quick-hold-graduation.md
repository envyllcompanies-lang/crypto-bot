## What it shows

All of the cell's profit, in both periods, comes from the 7-9% of trades still open when the curve completed, each worth about +56-59% when sold into the new pool. The other 91-93% of trades lost 2-3% each. The pre-registration's claim that the profit "is not carried by a few trades" was wrong. It wasn't caught because the search report's graduation check compared flags with "graduated" only, while pool-valued exits carry "graduated_pool": it read their share as 0 and left its section out (fixed in screen.py and optimize.py; `reports/2026-10-14-optimize-pool-d2.md` regenerated). Two more reasons to doubt those graduation values: the pool price falls back to the opening price where the swap data has gaps (whole days in early September; now flagged `graduated_pool_open` in new runs), and the leader list used on 1-23 Sep was chosen from wallets that profited on those same tokens (follow.py notes this), so the discovery half is not out of sample for the leader rule.
